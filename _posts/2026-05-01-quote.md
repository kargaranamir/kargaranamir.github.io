Over time, I have collected a few quotes that stay with me. They are not conclusions, but small reminders that shift meaning depending on the moment. Some I have traced back through others, while others I do not know where I first heard them. When I know the source, I include the name.

> “The tallest tree gets the wind.” — Zhuo Fan (The Steward Demonic Emperor)  
> This resonates with my understanding of a broader Chinese mindset that values modesty, balance, and staying low rather than seeking prominence.


> “Their memory serves as an example to us all!
> The courageous fallen! The anguished fallen!
> Their lives have meaning because we, the living, **refuse to forget them**!
> And as we ride to certain death, we trust our successors to do the same for us!”  - Erwin Smith (Shingeki no Kyojin)


> “A king never wavers, never bends, never relies on others, and never gives up.” Qin Shi Huang (Record of Ragnarok)

> “Wherever I sit, that place shall become my throne.” — Qin Shi Huang (Record of Ragnarok)

> “80 percent of success is showing up.” — usually attributed to Woody Allen

> “Drinking wine and being joyful is my creed;
> Being free from both unbelief and religion is my religion.
> I asked the Bride of Time: ‘What is your dowry?’
> She answered: ‘Your cheerful heart is my dowry.’” — Khayyam


> “What’s the biggest lie you were ever told?
> — It’s not that simple.
>  No, that’s the biggest lie I was ever told: ‘It’s not that simple.’
>  And it’s a lie they tell you over and over again.
>  What’s not simple? Any of it. All of it.
>  It’s how they get you to give up.”
>  — Vinny Pazienza (Bleed for This)


I return to these lines not for answers, but for perspective. Each time I read them, they feel slightly different. Some are attributed to real people, while others come from anime, manga, or manhwa, but I find meaning in all of them in the same way.
