I am a <b>Ph.D. student in Computer Science</b> at [<b>Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich</b>](https://cis.lmu.de), advised by Prof. [<b>Hinrich Schütze</b>](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qIL9dWUAAAAJ). I work on <b>natural language processing</b>, with a focus on multilingual settings.

My research focuses on making language technology work for the world's many languages, especially those that are low-resource or computationally invisible:

- **Language identification and multilingual data**: finding and cleaning text for thousands of languages ([GlotLID](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.410/), [GlotScript](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.687/), [GlotCC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23825), [FineWeb2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20920)).
- **The web**: crawling, indexing and understanding the web, from minority-language web data to ad and tracker detection ([GlotWeb](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3774904.3792887), [Wide-AdGraph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14826)).
- **Translation**: data and benchmarks for machine translation, especially beyond English ([FineTranslations](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/finetranslations), [Last Translation Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04173)).
- **How multilingual LLMs work inside**: how languages, concepts and facts are represented and shared ([MEXA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05873), [code neurons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01074)).
- **Evaluation and robustness**: where models fail, from unseen scripts to biased or unstable answers ([GlotOCR Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12978), [CoBia](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.84/), [Who Flips?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16011)).

I have interned at [<b>Hugging Face</b>](https://huggingface.co), [<b>Microsoft</b>](https://www.microsoft.com), and [<b>SAP</b>](https://www.sap.com), where I worked on machine learning and language technologies.

Outside my research, I lead [SIGIranic](https://github.com/sigiranic), an open-source community for Iranian languages. My favorite areas of math are graph theory and cryptography.
