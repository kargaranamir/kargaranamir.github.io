Over time, I have collected a few quotes and sayings that stay with me. They are not conclusions, but small reminders that shift meaning depending on the moment. Some are quotes from real people or from characters in anime, manga, manhwa, donghua and novels; others are proverbs or sayings I read or heard once or twice but whose source I no longer remember. When I know the source, I include the name.
