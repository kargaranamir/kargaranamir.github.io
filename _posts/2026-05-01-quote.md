      The courageous fallen! The anguished fallen!
      Their lives have meaning because we, the living, <b>refuse to forget them</b>!
      And as we ride to certain death, we trust our successors to do the same for us!
    who: Erwin Smith
    source: Attack on Titan (Shingeki no Kyojin)
  - text: "A king never wavers, never bends, never relies on others, and never gives up."
    who: Qin Shi Huang
    source: Record of Ragnarok
  - text: "Wherever I sit, that place shall become my throne."
    who: Qin Shi Huang
    source: Record of Ragnarok
  - text: "80 percent of success is showing up."
    who: usually attributed to Woody Allen
  - text: |
      Drinking wine and being joyful is my creed;
      Being free from both unbelief and religion is my religion.
      I asked the Bride of Time: ‘What is your dowry?’
      She answered: ‘Your cheerful heart is my dowry.’
    who: Omar Khayyam
  - text: |
      What’s the biggest lie you were ever told?
      — It’s not that simple.
      No, that’s the biggest lie I was ever told: ‘It’s not that simple.’
      And it’s a lie they tell you over and over again.
      What’s not simple? Any of it. All of it.
      It’s how they get you to give up.
    who: Vinny Pazienza
    source: Bleed for This
  - text: "Kids who have never seen peace and kids who have never seen war have different values."
    who: Donquixote Doflamingo

    who: usually attributed to Napoleon Bonaparte
    note: Often misattributed to Sun Tzu's *The Art of War*, where it does not appear.
  - text: "Why would I chase a glacier when there’s a perfectly good sunshine right in front of me?"
  - text: "Did I owe the entire Milky Way money to end up with this?"

I return to these lines not for answers, but for perspective. Each time I read them, they feel slightly different. Some are attributed to real people, while others come from anime, manga, or manhwa, but I find meaning in all of them in the same way.
